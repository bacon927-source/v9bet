Passkey 與 Authenticator

Passkeys and Authenticator Apps
兩件常被混為一談的事。Passkey 取代密碼——一步完成、擋得住釣魚。Authenticator 疊加在密碼之後——第二道關卡，但本身仍然可能被釣。市場走的路是：用 passkey 登入，用 authenticator 確認金流。
Two things routinely conflated. A passkey replaces the password — one step, phishing-resistant. An authenticator code adds to it — a second factor, still phishable in itself. Where the market has landed: passkeys for signing in, authenticators for confirming money moving.

市場上兩邊各自怎麼做

What the market has settled on for each
Method A

Passkey（WebAuthn / FIDO2）

Google · Apple · Microsoft · Amazon · PayPal · Shopify
何時問
登入成功之後才問，不在註冊時問。註冊時會員還沒有帳號，作業系統彈出「要為 v9bet 建立 passkey 嗎」對他毫無意義，而且會傷註冊轉換。
Offered after a successful login, never during registration — the OS sheet is meaningless before an account exists, and it costs conversion.
怎麼登入
不做「用 passkey 登入」按鈕，而是讓它出現在帳號欄位的自動填入清單裡。按鈕是猜測，自動填入只在真的有 passkey 時才出現。
No dedicated button — it surfaces in the identifier field's autofill. A button is a guess; autofill appears only when one actually exists.
文案
對會員說「用 Face ID 登入」，不說 passkey。Apple、Google、PayPal 都主打生物辨識，把 passkey 這個詞留在設定頁。
Say "sign in with Face ID", not "passkey". The standard's name belongs in Settings, where the member is already looking for a control.
數量
一台裝置一把，而且 iCloud 鑰匙圈與 Google 密碼管理工具會自動同步，所以一個人有好幾把是常態。不要限制數量，也不要因為久沒用就自動刪。
One per device, and iCloud Keychain / Google Password Manager sync them — several is normal. Never cap the count, never auto-expire for disuse.
底線
永遠保留退路。沒有任何一家做成只能用 passkey。生物辨識失敗、換手機、裝置不支援，都必須有另一條路。
Always keep a fallback. Nobody ships passkey-only — biometrics fail, phones change, devices vary.
Method B

Authenticator（TOTP）

Google Authenticator · Authy · 1Password · Microsoft
標準
RFC 6238，6 位數、30 秒，所有 app 都一樣，沒有專屬規格要設計。QR 內容是標準的 otpauth:// 連結。
RFC 6238 — six digits, 30-second window, identical across every app. The QR is a standard otpauth:// URI.
一定要有
手動輸入金鑰。最常見的情境是 app 就裝在同一支手機上，根本沒有第二個鏡頭能掃。
Manual key entry is not optional — the most common enrolment is on the same phone that holds the app, with no second camera.
一定要有
啟用前先驗一次碼。這一步會在會員還沒離開畫面時，抓到掃錯或時間不同步。
Verify one code before enabling — it catches a mis-scan or a wrong clock while the member is still on the screen.
一定要有
備援碼，只顯示一次、雜湊儲存，而且要求會員明確勾選「我已經存好了」。這是整段流程真正的重點。
Recovery codes — shown once, stored hashed, with an explicit "I've saved these". This is the actual point of the flow.
趨勢
市場正在把 TOTP 從「每次登入都要」退回「只在動到錢或設定時要」。它是每次登入的摩擦，而且本身擋不住釣魚。
The direction of travel is away from TOTP on every login and toward step-up only — it is friction each time and phishable anyway.

放到 v9bet 身上

What this means for this product specifically
Passkey 已經有了
Passkey already exists
登入頁的生物辨識分頁、Setup 項目 4、Settings 的 Passkeys 管理都已經在規格裡。新的 wireframe 補的是登入後的推薦時機、自動填入登入、跨裝置 QR，以及「不能移除最後一種登入方式」的擋關。
The biometric login tab, Setup item 4 and the Passkeys list are already specified. The new wireframe adds the post-login upsell, autofill sign-in, cross-device QR, and the guard that refuses to remove the last sign-in method.
Authenticator 的價值在提款
The authenticator earns its place at withdrawal
目前提款 step-up 走 email／SMS OTP。改用 authenticator 有兩個實際好處：擋得住 SIM swap（這在越南泰國是真實威脅），而且啟用後每次都免費，不像簡訊每則都要成本。
Withdrawal step-up currently uses email/SMS OTP. An authenticator is SIM-swap proof — a live threat in these markets — and costs nothing per use, unlike SMS.
不要兩個一起問
Never stack both
有 authenticator 的人，提款就只問 app 的碼，不要同時再寄一封 email。兩道關卡疊在同一個動作上，不是雙倍安全，是雙倍放棄率。沒啟用的人完全照現在走，流程不變。
With one enrolled, the withdrawal asks for the app's code and nothing else. Two factors on one action is not twice the security, it is twice the drop-off. Members without one see no change at all.
金額要顯示在確認頁
Show the amount on the confirmation
step-up 畫面上必須同時看得到金額和收款帳戶。只寫「請輸入驗證碼」等於叫會員在空白紙上簽名——真的被盜用時，是他自己按下核准的。
The step-up screen must show the amount and destination. "Enter your code" alone is a signature on a blank page — if the session were hijacked, the member approves it themselves.

一個這個產品特有的硬性限制 · A hard constraint specific to this product

沒有已驗證聯絡方式的帳號，不能啟用 authenticator。用 Zalo 註冊、沒有 email 也沒有電話的會員，如果再啟用 authenticator 然後手機掉了——沒有任何管道可以證明身分，備援碼是唯一的路，而備援碼也在那支手機上的機率很高。這不是建議，是要在畫面上直接擋下來，沿用 Setup Step 1 那個 has_verified_contact 判斷。
An account with no verified contact must not be allowed to enrol. A Zalo sign-up with no email and no phone, plus an authenticator on a phone that is later lost, has no way to prove ownership — recovery codes are the only route, and they are most likely on that same phone. This is a block on the screen, not advice, reusing the has_verified_contact test from Setup Step 1.

建議的順序

Suggested order of work
  1. 先把 passkey 補完。它已經有基礎，而且是純粹的體驗改善——登入從「等驗證碼」變成一秒，沒有人需要學新東西。登入後推薦的時機是最大的一塊。
    Finish passkeys first. The groundwork exists, and it is pure experience gain — login drops from waiting for a code to one second, with nothing new to learn. The post-login upsell is the biggest piece.
  2. Authenticator 當成提款的選配強化。不要放進 Setup——那裡已經九項了，而且新會員還沒有錢可以提，這時候叫他裝一個 app 是最糟的時機。入口放 Settings › Security。
    Treat the authenticator as optional hardening for withdrawal. Keep it out of Setup — nine items is enough, and a brand-new member with no money to withdraw is the worst possible moment to ask them to install an app. Entry point: Settings › Security.
  3. 備援碼那一步不能省。沒有存下備援碼的 authenticator，等於把「手機掉了」變成一張客服單，而博弈帳號的身分驗證沒辦法在聊天室裡完成。
    Do not cut the recovery-code step. An authenticator without saved codes converts a lost phone into a support ticket, and identity for a gambling account cannot be verified over chat.
