錢的三條路

Three Roads for the Money
一個 Banking 畫面、兩條存款路、一條提款路。整套流程只有一個真相來源——金流商的 server callback，前端永遠只是在等；而錢包模型只有一條鐵律：存進來的錢永不上鎖，被鎖的只有紅利。
One Banking screen, two deposit roads, one withdrawal road. The whole flow has a single source of truth — the provider's server callback; the front end only ever waits. And the wallet model has one iron rule: deposited money is never locked — only the bonus is.

三條路各自的規矩

What each road runs on
Road ① · deposit

手動轉帳路

Manual — bank transfer / USDT
pay → reconcile by note
我們給資料，會員在自己的銀行或錢包 app 付款，靠唯一的轉帳備註對帳。所以這條路沒有「取消存款」——資料已經在會員手上，他隨時可以照著付，遲到的款項照樣被對上。
We hand over the details, the member pays in their own bank or wallet app, and we reconcile by the unique transfer note. Which is why this road has no "cancel deposit" — the member already holds the details and can still pay; a late payment still gets matched.

Road ② · deposit

金流商路

Gateway — direct bank / QR / e-wallet
handoff → webhook
交棒給第三方：redirect 整頁離開，或 iframe 留在我們的框裡（銀行拒框就自動退回 redirect，不准顯示空箱子）。結果只來自 server-to-server webhook；會員沒回來不等於取消，單子留在 pending 等 callback。
Hand off to a third party: a full redirect, or an iframe inside our chrome (if the bank refuses framing, fall back to redirect — never render an empty box). The outcome comes only from the server-to-server webhook; not returning is not a cancel — the deposit stays pending until the callback lands.

Road ③ · withdrawal

提款路

Withdrawal — the only gated road
account → OTP? → review
唯一有關卡的一條，而且每道關卡都講理：可提＝自己的錢 − pending；戶名鎖定本人；首次使用的收款帳戶才出 OTP；有紅利時警告沒收、不阻擋；駁回必附理由、退回餘額、連到修法。
The only road with gates, and every gate explains itself: withdrawable = own money − pending; the holder name is locked to the profile; OTP appears only for a first-time payout account; an active bonus warns about forfeiture, never blocks; a rejection carries the reason, the refund, and the fix.

整個錢包共用的規則

Rules that hold across the whole wallet
存款永不上鎖
The deposit is never locked
被鎖的只有紅利和紅利贏來的錢。餘額因此拆成自己的（隨時可提）＋ 紅利（鎖到 turnover 達標）兩個數字分開顯示。提早提款是「沒收紅利」的交易，不是被拒絕的請求——畫面警告、要求確認，但不阻擋。每個能領紅利的地方都要講清楚鎖的是什麼：說過頭跟說不足一樣糟，以為本金被凍結的會員會停止存款。
Only the bonus and what it wins are locked. The balance therefore splits into own money (always withdrawable) + bonus (locked until turnover), shown as two numbers. Withdrawing early is a forfeit-the-bonus trade, not a refused request — the screen warns and asks to confirm, never blocks. Everywhere a bonus can be accepted must say exactly what locks: overstating is as bad as understating, because members who think their deposit froze stop depositing.
Turnover 只管提款
Turnover is withdrawal-side only
churn 管的是能拿出去多少，永遠不管能放進來多少。未達標、紅利進行中、紅利快過期——都不准擋存款、不准加步驟、不准跳警告。唯一能擋住一筆存款的是方法層：維護中、min/max、市場開放。維護中的方法畫成虛線不可點、寫明結束時間，絕不無聲消失。
Churn governs what can leave, never what can enter. Unmet turnover, an active bonus, an expiring bonus — none may block, warn on, or add a step to a deposit. The only thing that stops one is the method layer: maintenance, min/max, market availability. A method under maintenance renders dashed and un-tappable with its end time stated — never silently missing.
成功只來自 callback
Success comes only from the callback
前端永遠讀不到結果：iframe 跨域、redirect 回程可被偽造或中斷。POST /webhooks/deposit 是唯一的真相來源，UI 的工作是說「我們在等」，不是猜。所以回到站上的畫面叫 confirming，它自己會變成 Credited 或留在 Pending——等的是 callback，不是會員。
The front end can never read the outcome: the iframe is cross-origin, and a redirect return can be faked or interrupted. POST /webhooks/deposit is the only source of truth; the UI's job is to say "we're waiting", not to guess. That's why the return screen is called confirming — it resolves into Credited or stays Pending on its own, waiting for the callback, not the member.
重試安全看階段
Retry safety depends on the stage
交棒前失敗（session 建不起來）＝沒有錢在路上，可以重試，但要換新的 reference。交棒後失聯＝付款可能已經成功，重試就是付兩次——只給「查狀態」和 Live Chat。兩種狀態絕不能共用一顆通用的 Try again。會員沒回來也不是失敗：他可能只是關了分頁，單子照常在 callback 完成。
A failure before handoff (the session never got created) means no money is in flight — retry is safe, with a new reference. Losing contact after handoff means the payment may have succeeded — a retry risks paying twice, so that state offers only "check status" and Live Chat. The two states must never share one generic Try again. And not returning is not a failure: the member may have just closed the tab; the deposit completes on the callback.
一筆交易，三個視圖，一個字串
One transaction, three views, one string
同一筆存款有三個追蹤處，刻意不合併：開著的 Status 畫面、離開後的 Live Status Bubble（status-bubble-wireframe.html）、永久的 History 類別 1（history-flow-wireframe.html）——三者讀同一個 status 欄位。reference 在這裡、History、Live Chat 顯示同一個字串，會員在哪裡都能引用。Status 的每一個狀態都連 History：這個畫面是 moment，不是 place。
One deposit has three places to follow it, deliberately unmerged: the open Status screen, the Live Status Bubble after leaving (status-bubble-wireframe.html), and the permanent History category 1 (history-flow-wireframe.html) — all reading the same status field. The reference renders as the same string here, in History and in Live Chat, quotable anywhere. Every Status state links into History: this screen is a moment, not a place.

這條流程還缺三件事 · Three things this flow still does not specify

  1. 會員取消提款的入口沒畫。spec 裡有「cancelled by member (while in Review)」狀態和 DELETE /withdrawal/{id}，但 Status 畫面上沒有任何取消按鈕或確認步驟——狀態存在，路卻不存在。要嘛畫出來（只在 Review 階段出現），要嘛把狀態和 API 拿掉。
    The member-cancel entry point is not drawn. The spec lists a "cancelled by member (while in Review)" state and DELETE /withdrawal/{id}, but the Status screen has no cancel control or confirm step — the state exists, the path doesn't. Either draw it (visible only during Review) or remove the state and the API.
  2. USDT 匯率過期之後的事沒定義。鎖匯 15 分鐘、quote expired → re-quote 都有，但 re-quote 的畫面沒畫；更麻煩的是「I'll do this later」之後遲到的 USDT——spec 說落入 unmatched 人工對帳，那用哪個匯率入帳？匯率爭議是 USDT 通道最可預期的客服單，不能留白。
    What happens after the USDT rate expires is undefined. The 15-minute lock and the quote-expired → re-quote state exist, but the re-quote screen is not drawn; worse, a late USDT payment after "I'll do this later" falls to unmatched manual reconciliation — at which rate does it credit? Rate disputes are the most predictable support ticket on the USDT rail; this cannot stay blank.
  3. 手續費規則整套缺席。提款確認畫面寫死「Fee ₫0」。哪些方法收費、超過幾次免費、誰吸收、VN 與 TH 是否同規——規格裡沒有任何一處回答。如果永遠免費，就該寫成規則而不是一個寫死的數字；如果不是，費率表要在送出前讓會員看到。
    The fee schedule is entirely absent. The withdrawal confirm screen hardcodes "Fee ₫0". Which methods charge, how many free withdrawals, who absorbs it, whether VN and TH differ — nothing in the kit answers any of it. If it's free forever, write that as a rule rather than a hardcoded number; if not, the schedule must be visible before submit.
