一條時間軸，七個類別

One Timeline, Seven Categories
History 不是日誌，是「我的錢去哪了」的答案頁。所有事件進同一條時間軸，用七個類別篩；前端永遠只載入 30 天，更舊的走匯出；而每一列壞消息都必須說出為什麼，並把人送去能修好它的地方。
History is not a log — it is the answer page for "where did my money go". Everything lands on one timeline filtered by seven categories; the front end only ever loads 30 days, export covers the rest; and every row of bad news has to say why, then route to the place that fixes it.

整條時間軸共用的規則

Rules that hold across the whole timeline
30 天是視窗，不是保留期
A window, not retention
前端一律只載入最近 30 天（08-30 決定），所以日期篩選只有 7d／30d／自訂 ≤30d。保留期是另一個數字：按類別、由法規決定（金流與投注 5–7 年）。過了 30 天，紀錄離開的是 app 的清單，不是系統——爭議、退款、AML 全靠它還在。
The front end loads a uniform rolling 30 days (08-30), so the date filter offers 7d / 30d / custom ≤30d. Retention is a different number: per category, set by regulation (money and bets 5–7 years). Past 30 days a record leaves the app's list, not the system — disputes, chargebacks and AML all depend on that.
P&L 是唯一例外
The one deliberate exception
投注 P&L 保留 7d／30d／90d／All——因為那是伺服器算好的聚合數字，不是載入舊紀錄。負責任博弈就靠它回答「我今年輸了多少」；把這頁「修正」成 30 天等於拆掉 RG 的地基。規則管的是紀錄清單，不是統計。
Bet P&L keeps 7d / 30d / 90d / All — those are server-computed aggregates, not old rows loaded into the app. Responsible Gaming depends on it to answer "how much have I lost this year"; "fixing" this screen to 30 days would pull the floor out from under RG. The rule governs record lists, not statistics.
壞消息必附原因與下一步
Bad news carries why + next
被拒的提款寫明 churn 還差多少、連到進度頁；退件的 KYC 給一鍵重交；沒收的紅利說是提早提款還是過期。History 的工作是取代客服單，做不到這點它就只是一份日誌。
A rejected withdrawal states how much churn remains and links to the progress page; a bounced KYC document gets one-tap re-submission; a forfeited bonus says whether it was early withdrawal or expiry. History's job is to replace the support ticket — without this it is just a log.
Ask AI 只讀自己、答案必附紀錄
Own data only, and it cites
只能查自己的 History，不能動作（不能用聊天取消提款）。每個答案附上它引用的篩選，View these N transactions 重跑一模一樣的查詢——沒有驗證不了的黑盒數字。它跟 AI Live Chat 是兩個入口：那邊是找客服，這邊是查自己，不要合併。
It reads only the member's own History and takes no actions (no cancelling a withdrawal via chat). Every answer carries the filter behind it, and View these N transactions re-runs the identical query — no unverifiable black-box numbers. It is a separate surface from AI Live Chat: that one talks to support, this one queries your own data. Keep the entries apart.
會員看到的是子集
The member sees a subset
安全事件給會員看動作、時間、驗證通道與遮罩後的聯絡方式；IP、失敗明細、OTP 內部細節只在後台稽核庫。同一筆推薦獎勵在類別 3 和 5 各出現一次——同一紀錄、兩個視圖，永不重複計。
Security events show the member the action, time, channel and masked contact; raw IP, failure forensics and OTP internals stay in the back-office audit store. The same referral reward appears in both category 3 and 5 — one record, two views, never double-counted.

這條流程還缺三件事 · Three things this flow still does not specify

  1. 捲到 30 天盡頭的畫面沒畫。無限捲動總會到底，但「到底了——更舊的請匯出」這個狀態不存在。沒有它，會員只會以為紀錄不見了，而這正是 30 天視窗最容易被誤讀成「刪掉了」的地方。
    The end of the 30-day scroll is not drawn. Infinite scroll eventually hits bottom, but the "that's everything — older records via export" state doesn't exist. Without it a member just assumes records vanished — exactly where the 30-day window gets misread as deletion.
  2. 匯出的安全沒定義。CSV 裝著多年的金流，異步寄進收件匣：要不要 OTP step-up？下載連結效期多長？Message Center 只留 30 天，連結過期後走哪裡？匯出是整套 30 天視窗的洩壓閥，它的門檻不能是空白。
    Export security is undefined. The CSV holds years of money movement and arrives async via the inbox: does requesting it need an OTP step-up? How long does the link live? Message Center keeps 30 days — what happens after the link expires? Export is the pressure valve of the whole 30-day window; its gate cannot be blank.
  3. 保留年限全是提案。5–7 年、2 年+、「帳號存續期」都標著待法務確認——VN 與 TH 各自確認之前，後端的 TTL 與歸檔排程一個都不能上。這是整張矩陣裡唯一擋 Dev 的格子。
    Every retention figure is a proposal. The 5–7 years, 2 years+, "account life" all carry "confirm with legal per market" — until VN and TH each confirm, none of the backend TTL / archival jobs can ship. It is the one cell in the matrix that actually blocks Dev.
