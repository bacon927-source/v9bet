進站三條路

Three Ways In
註冊和登入是同一個畫面的兩個分頁，而且在驗證碼通過之前，根本分不出是哪一條路。分岔點不是會員按了哪個分頁，而是驗證通過後帳號是什麼狀態：新建、正常、休眠、還是受限。
Sign up and Login are two tabs on one screen, and until the code passes there is no way to tell which route this is. The fork is not which tab was tapped — it is what state the account turns out to be in: new, active, dormant, or restricted.

What each route actually is

The only route that creates an account. Passing the code creates it with an auto-generated Account ID, then goes straight into Setup.

四種結果共用的規則

Rules that hold across every outcome
驗證前不透露
Nothing before the code
帳號存不存在、是正常、休眠還是受限，在驗證通過之前一律看不出來。註冊頁是唯一的例外——那裡會明講 email 已註冊，是業務決定，所以必須靠限流補上。
Whether an account exists, and whether it is active, dormant or restricted, is invisible until the code passes. The sign-up screen is the deliberate exception, which is why rate limiting carries the weight there.
算哪一種看結果
The outcome decides
不看從哪個畫面進來。只有 created 算註冊；signed_in 和 reactivated 都是登入，前者帶 entry_point，後者另外標記。從註冊頁走完驗證碼就送 sign_up，會把本來就有帳號的人算進新客數——而這些人正是最容易走這條路的。
Not which screen they came from. Only created is a registration; signed_in and reactivated are both logins. Firing sign_up for everyone completing a code on the sign-up form inflates new customers with people who already had accounts — and those are exactly the people who take that route.
Setup 只跑一次
Setup runs once
只有新建立的帳號會被推進 Setup。② 和 ③ 都直接進站。唯一例外是當年沒做完 Step 1 就離開的人，回來仍要補完才能存款或遊戲。
Only a newly created account is pushed into Setup. Routes ② and ③ go straight in. The one exception is someone who abandoned before finishing Step 1 — they still have to complete it before depositing or playing.
受限是死路
Restricted is a dead end
自我排除、RG 封鎖、合規凍結一律只給 Live Chat，不說原因、沒有自助管道。這不是體驗瑕疵——自我排除的人第一個會試的就是重新註冊，這條路必須走不通。
Self-exclusion, RG blocks and compliance holds all stop at Live Chat with no reason given and no self-service route. That is not a rough edge — re-registering is the first thing a self-excluded member tries, and it has to fail.

休眠這條路，規格還缺三件事 · Three things the dormant route still does not specify

  1. 多久算休眠？整套規格從頭到尾用「dormant」，但沒有任何一處定義門檻。一年？半年？這是後端要跑排程的數字，也決定 Route ③ 會有多少人。
    After how long? The whole kit says "dormant" without ever defining the threshold. A year? Six months? The backend needs a number to schedule against, and it decides how many members Route ③ actually has.
  2. 解凍需不需要合規複查？目前畫的是「原地重新啟用、什麼都不變」。但博弈帳號長期休眠後回來，很多市場要求重新做 KYC 或風險評估。如果要加，要加在解凍那一步，不是等到提款才擋。
    Does unfreezing need a compliance re-check? It is currently drawn as reactivate-in-place with nothing changed. Many markets require re-KYC or a fresh risk assessment when a long-dormant gambling account returns. If that applies, it belongs at the unfreeze step, not deferred to the first withdrawal.
  3. 凍結期間餘額怎麼處理？畫面上寫「餘額保留」。有些司法管轄區對長期未動用的帳戶有管理費或無人認領資產的規定。如果有，會員必須在解凍畫面上看到，不能讓他自己去對帳。
    What happens to the balance while frozen? The screen says it is preserved. Some jurisdictions impose dormancy fees or unclaimed-property rules. If any apply, the member has to see it on the reactivation screen rather than discover it by checking their own balance.
