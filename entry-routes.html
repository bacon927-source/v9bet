進站三條路

Three Ways In
註冊和登入是同一個畫面的兩個分頁，而且在驗證碼通過之前，根本分不出是哪一條路。分岔點不是會員按了哪個分頁，而是驗證通過後帳號是什麼狀態：新建、正常、休眠、黑名單、還是受限。
Sign up and Login are two tabs on one screen, and until the code passes there is no way to tell which route this is. The fork is not which tab was tapped — it is what state the account turns out to be in: new, active, dormant, blacklisted, or restricted.

四條路各自是什麼

What each route actually is — plus the one that is kept out
Route ①

註冊

Registration — the only one
sign_up
唯一會建立帳號的一條。Email 送出後寄 6 位數碼，通過就建立帳號、自動產生 Account ID，接著強制進 Setup。
The only route that creates an account. Passing the code creates it with an auto-generated Account ID, then goes straight into Setup.
Setup
8 項，只有 Step 1 擋人
結束
Welcome ＋ 優惠 → 教學
埋點
帶 method 與 referral_source
Route ②

登入

Login — the account is active
login
帳號一直是正常的，驗證通過就直接進站。不建立帳號、不跑 Setup、沒有任何額外步驟。
The account was active all along; passing verification goes straight in. Nothing created, no Setup, no extra step.
入口
登入頁，或在註冊頁打了已註冊的 email
裝置
同時只能一台，新登入會踢掉舊的
埋點
從註冊頁進來的要帶 entry_point
Route ③

重新啟用

Reactivation — the account was dormant
login · reactivated
長期沒登入而被休眠的帳號。驗證方式跟登入一樣，但多了一個解凍動作——這是它跟 Route ② 真正的差別，不是跟註冊的差別。
An account made dormant by long inactivity. Verification is identical to login, but an unfreeze happens first — that is the real difference from Route ②, not from registration.
保留
餘額、紀錄、等級、收款帳戶全部不動
不重跑
Setup 不會再來一次
埋點
必須獨立標記，否則喚回成效看不見
Route ④

黑名單

Blacklisted — in, but limited
login · blacklisted
進得來，不是被擋在門外。照常登入、照常用站，但有一組被定義好的功能不給用。受限管的是「能不能進來」，黑名單管的是「進來之後能做什麼」。
They get in — this is not a block. Normal sign-in, normal use, with a defined set of features withheld. Restricted is about access; blacklisted is about capability.
文案
只說「部分功能無法使用」，不列是哪些、不說原因
待補
停用哪些功能是風控的決定，規格還沒有這份清單
待決
功能是隱藏，還是顯示但停用並說明

五種結果共用的規則

Rules that hold across every outcome
驗證前不透露
Nothing before the code
帳號存不存在、是正常、休眠還是受限，在驗證通過之前一律看不出來。註冊頁是唯一的例外——那裡會明講 email 已註冊，是業務決定，所以必須靠限流補上。
Whether an account exists, and whether it is active, dormant or restricted, is invisible until the code passes. The sign-up screen is the deliberate exception, which is why rate limiting carries the weight there.
算哪一種看結果
The outcome decides
不看從哪個畫面進來。只有 created 算註冊；signed_in 和 reactivated 都是登入，前者帶 entry_point，後者另外標記。從註冊頁走完驗證碼就送 sign_up，會把本來就有帳號的人算進新客數——而這些人正是最容易走這條路的。
Not which screen they came from. Only created is a registration; signed_in and reactivated are both logins. Firing sign_up for everyone completing a code on the sign-up form inflates new customers with people who already had accounts — and those are exactly the people who take that route.
Setup 只跑一次
Setup runs once
只有新建立的帳號會被推進 Setup。②③④ 都直接進站。唯一例外是當年沒做完 Step 1 就離開的人，回來仍要補完才能存款或遊戲。
Only a newly created account is pushed into Setup. Routes ②, ③ and ④ go straight in. The one exception is someone who abandoned before finishing Step 1 — they still have to complete it before depositing or playing.
終點是首頁，不是教學
The flow ends on Home
Welcome 之後先到首頁，教學從首頁才出現。註冊應該結束在產品上，不是結束在一段導覽。而且教學不是只給新會員的——功能一直在上新的，舊會員也需要被告知。同一個元件、不同觸發：新會員看基本操作，回來的人看「上次之後多了什麼」，兩邊都不會看到已經看過的東西。
Welcome leads to Home first, and the tour appears from there. Registration should finish on the product, not on a walkthrough. And the tour is not only for new members — the product keeps shipping, and existing customers need telling. One component, two triggers: newcomers see the basics, returning members see what has landed since, and neither sees anything twice.
受限是死路
Restricted is a dead end
自我排除、RG 封鎖、合規凍結一律只給 Live Chat，不說原因、沒有自助管道。這不是體驗瑕疵——自我排除的人第一個會試的就是重新註冊，這條路必須走不通。
Self-exclusion, RG blocks and compliance holds all stop at Live Chat with no reason given and no self-service route. That is not a rough edge — re-registering is the first thing a self-excluded member tries, and it has to fail.

規格還缺的五件事 · Five things still unspecified

  1. 黑名單停用哪些功能？規格現在只知道「有一組功能不給用」，清單本身還沒有。這是風控的決定，但不拿到清單就畫不出畫面——提款？優惠？特定遊戲？每一種的擋法和文案都不一樣。
    Which features does a blacklist withhold? The spec knows there is a withheld set but not what is in it. That call belongs to risk control, and without the list the screens cannot be drawn — withdrawal, promotions, certain games all block differently and read differently.
  2. 被停用的功能是隱藏還是停用？隱藏比較乾淨，但客服會很難處理——會員講不出自己少了什麼。這要一次決定、全站一致，不要一個功能一種做法。
    Hide the feature, or show it disabled? Hiding is cleaner but makes support harder, since the member cannot point at what is missing. Decide once and apply it everywhere, rather than per feature.
  3. 多久算休眠？整套規格從頭到尾用「dormant」，但沒有任何一處定義門檻。一年？半年？這是後端要跑排程的數字，也決定 Route ③ 會有多少人。
    After how long? The whole kit says "dormant" without ever defining the threshold. A year? Six months? The backend needs a number to schedule against, and it decides how many members Route ③ actually has.
  4. 解凍需不需要合規複查？目前畫的是「原地重新啟用、什麼都不變」。但博弈帳號長期休眠後回來，很多市場要求重新做 KYC 或風險評估。如果要加，要加在解凍那一步，不是等到提款才擋。
    Does unfreezing need a compliance re-check? It is currently drawn as reactivate-in-place with nothing changed. Many markets require re-KYC or a fresh risk assessment when a long-dormant gambling account returns. If that applies, it belongs at the unfreeze step, not deferred to the first withdrawal.
  5. 凍結期間餘額怎麼處理？畫面上寫「餘額保留」。有些司法管轄區對長期未動用的帳戶有管理費或無人認領資產的規定。如果有，會員必須在解凍畫面上看到，不能讓他自己去對帳。
    What happens to the balance while frozen? The screen says it is preserved. Some jurisdictions impose dormancy fees or unclaimed-property rules. If any apply, the member has to see it on the reactivation screen rather than discover it by checking their own balance.
